## Changes from the originals

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-11-11. Each download's SHA-1
matched the one Commons lists.

| File | Original (SHA-1) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `victor.png` | `CosmosLaundromatVictor.jpg`, 894×853 (`81522437ca72fa12a42007d7a94dc0c2af710fbd`) | None (re-encoded as PNG) |
| `spring.png` | `Blender Open Movie - Spring (2019).png`, 1920×804 (`22f5f6dad11112b00c289f6bbd4e08c84ce2b2fd`) | Cropped to rows 48–739 to remove the media player's title bar and controls |
| `sintel.png` | `Sintel poster.jpg`, 905×1280 (`60b941b782aeafb4bc1a93fdcc2fb7fe36882b01`) | Cropped to rows 70–1044: removes the top line of text and the credits and logos |
| `bbb-alpha.png` | `Bbb-splash.png`, 1920×1080 (`929c8e669a8cd64471b5c9f49ae8be1c2b8152a8`) | Scaled to 1280×720 (bicubic); alpha channel added |

The crops and the scale were done once with Windows' System.Drawing
(`Graphics.DrawImage`, high-quality bicubic, source-copy compositing) into
24-bit BMP, then `python tools/reference/make_stills.py <bmp folder>` wrote
the PNGs (8-bit, no color-space chunks).
